# Data Sources

publicly available macroeconomic data from Federal Reserve Economic Data (FRED).

## Initial Series

### Bank Credit to Private Non-Financial Sector
FRED Series ID: `QUSPBM770A`

Measures bank credit provided to the private non-financial sector relative to GDP.

### Domestic Nonfinancial Debt
FRED Series ID: `TCMDODNS`

Used alongside GDP data to examine the scale of domestic nonfinancial debt relative to economic output.

### Gross Domestic Product
FRED Series ID: `GDP`

Used as a measure of aggregate U.S. economic output and for normalization where required.

### Finance and Insurance Value Added
FRED Series ID: `VAPGDPFI`

Used to examine the economic weight of finance and insurance within the broader U.S. economy.
